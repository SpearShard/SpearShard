ABOUT

I’m a software engineer who likes building things from a blank screen all the way to something people can actually use.

My sweet spot is full-stack development, especially when engineering meets product design. I enjoy working across the stack, building polished interfaces, connecting them to real systems, and figuring out how to make the whole thing feel fast and intuitive.

Right now, a lot of that energy goes into RockSpace — building digital products, experimenting with ideas, and turning rough concepts into working software.
